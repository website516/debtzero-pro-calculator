About DebtZero Pro

DebtZero Pro was built to help people in Hong Kong take control of their debt using proven strategies — the Debt Snowball and Debt Avalanche methods.

Why We Built This

Many people in Hong Kong carry high-interest credit card debt but lack clear tools to compare repayment strategies. We created this calculator to make the decision simple and transparent.

Our Approach

We focus on clarity and honesty. The calculator shows real numbers based on your inputs, and we clearly explain the assumptions behind every calculation.
